Frequently asked questions

Learn more about PitHub, how to get started, and how to organize and share academic resources with the Lewis University community.

What is PitHub?

PitHub is a centralized academic resource hub developed for the Lewis University community. It allows faculty and staff to upload, organize, manage, and share instructional videos and documents in one place.

Who can register for PitHub?

PitHub accounts are currently available to Lewis University faculty and staff. Prospective users must complete the PitHub Registration Form . Once their registration is reviewed and their account is created using their Lewis University email address, they can sign in and access the platform.

Do students need a PitHub account to access shared resources?

No. Students and other recipients do not need a PitHub account to access shared content. Faculty can copy and distribute direct links to their uploaded videos or documents, allowing students to access the resources without signing in to PitHub.

What types of files can I upload?

PitHub supports instructional videos and common document formats, including PDF, DOCX, PPTX, and other commonly used educational file types. These resources can be uploaded and organized within classes or playlists.

How are resources organized in PitHub?

Users must first create a class or playlist before uploading instructional materials. Classes and playlists provide a way to group related videos and documents, making resources easier to manage and access.

What is the difference between a class and a playlist?

Classes and playlists both allow users to organize instructional resources. Classes can be configured as either public or private, while playlists are always private. Playlist owners can invite other PitHub users and assign permissions to control how invited members interact with the playlist's content.

Can I control who has access to my content?

Yes. PitHub provides public and private visibility options for classes. Public classes can be discovered by other users through the platform, while private classes restrict access.

Playlists are always private. Their owners can invite other registered PitHub users and assign permissions such as:

  • View Only: Members can view playlist content.
  • Post: Members can upload content to the playlist.
  • Delete: Members can also delete playlist content.
How can I share videos and documents with students?

The primary way to share resources with students is by copying the direct link to an uploaded video or document and distributing it through your preferred communication or learning management platform.

Students can open shared links without creating a PitHub account or signing in. This makes it easy to distribute instructional materials without requiring students to become PitHub users.

Can I search for other classes, documents, or users?

Yes. PitHub includes search functionality that allows users to look for classes, specific documents, and other users. Publicly accessible classes and user profiles can be discovered through the platform. Private content remains subject to its access settings.

Can I edit or delete content after uploading it?

Yes. Users can edit and delete their own uploaded content after it has been added to PitHub. This allows faculty and staff to maintain their resources and keep instructional materials up to date.

Do I need to create a class or playlist before uploading?

Yes. Before uploading a video or document, you must first create a class or playlist. Uploaded resources are organized within these collections rather than being uploaded independently.

What technologies power PitHub?

PitHub is built using React for its frontend interface and Firebase services for authentication, application data management, and file storage. GitHub is used for source control and development collaboration.

Where can I learn more about the development team?

Visit the Team page to learn more about the developers who originally created PitHub and the team currently maintaining and developing the platform.

How do I get started with PitHub?

To get started, complete the PitHub Registration Form . Once your account has been registered using your Lewis University email, you can sign in, create a class or playlist, and begin uploading and sharing instructional resources.
