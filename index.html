WHAT IS PITHUB?

A shared academic resource hub

PitHub is a web application designed for the Lewis University community. It helps faculty and staff upload and keep course-related videos and documents organized and easier to find.

Resources can be grouped into classes or playlists. Uploaders can control whether resources are public or private and manage who can access shared content.

THE CHALLENGE

Keeping instructional resources accessible shouldn't be complicated.

As educational platforms and services evolve, faculty need a dependable way to host, organize, and share their instructional materials. Maintaining access to these resources shouldn't mean compromising convenience, organization, or control.

01

Reliable content hosting

Lewis University faculty need a dependable place to host instructional videos, documents, and other educational materials for their courses.

02

Centralized course resources

Keeping instructional materials organized in one place makes it easier for faculty to manage content and for students to access the resources they need.

03

Control over content sharing

Faculty need flexibility in managing their instructional materials, controlling access, and deciding how resources are shared with their intended audiences.

HOW IT WORKS

1

Request Access

Complete the PitHub Registration Form to express your interest in using the platform.

2

Get Registered

Once your request is reviewed and approved, your Lewis University email will be registered for PitHub access.

3

Sign In & Get Started

Log in using your registered Lewis University account to access PitHub and its features.

4

Create & Upload

Create a class or playlist, then upload and organize your instructional videos and documents.

5

Share Your Resources

Share links to your uploaded content with students, or let others discover publicly accessible classes, documents, and profiles through PitHub.
